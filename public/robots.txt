# NostrFeed
#
# Notes, profiles and articles live at the root of the site under their NIP-19
# identifier — /npub1…, /note1…, /naddr1…. These were disallowed here on the
# grounds that their content is fetched from relays and so a crawl finds an
# empty page.
#
# That reasoning cost more than it saved. Twitterbot, Slackbot, LinkedIn and
# most other link-preview fetchers read robots.txt and honour it, so the rule
# was not keeping thin pages out of an index — it was stopping every shared
# link to a profile or an article from showing a preview at all. Google renders
# JavaScript and sees the real content; anything that does not is served the
# card tags in the HTML.
#
# What each page wants is decided by the page, through its robots meta tag.

User-agent: *
Allow: /

# Signed-in territory. Nothing here is another person's business, and a search
# result promising somebody's wallet or messages is a bad result.
Disallow: /settings
Disallow: /notifications
Disallow: /chat
Disallow: /bookmarks
Disallow: /wallet
Disallow: /identity
